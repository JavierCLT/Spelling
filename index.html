How this works (Parent Guide)

What it is: A speech‑to‑print spelling routine using Elkonin bars (one bar per sound), explicit grapheme‑phoneme mapping (digraphs & vowel teams), and immediate corrective feedback.

  • Bars = sounds (phonemes), not letters. e.g., ship → /sh/ /i/ /p/ → 3 bars.
  • Two letters can spell one sound (digraphs & vowel teams: sh, ch, th, wh, ck, ng, tch, ph, ai, ee, ea, oa, oo). Once a chunk is spelled, the whole bar turns green.
  • Strict keyboard: one wrong letter locks input; Backspace to fix; Enter/F1 re‑play audio.
  • Audio: local MP3 preferred for consistency; Web Speech TTS fallback.
  • Who for? K–1 (and older learners who need decoding/spelling practice). Adults pick a level and set a small goal (N words, ≤ M errors).
  • Tips: Have your child tap sounds, say each sound as they type, and use Backspace to correct immediately. Praise precision more than speed.

Science‑of‑Reading aligned: phoneme awareness → phonics (GPC) → spelling with corrective feedback.
